About Free Tuition Media

Making tutor matching simple, fair and free.

We started Free Tuition Media because finding a good tutor in Bangladesh took too many phone calls, too many middlemen, and too much guesswork. So we built a platform where guardians, students and tutors connect directly — no fees to post, no fees to search.

What We Stand For

Our values.

Verified, always

Every tutor completes an identity check before their profile goes live, so guardians can trust who they're inviting home.

Free for everyone

Posting a requirement and browsing tutor profiles costs nothing — we believe access to education shouldn't have a toll booth.

Fast matching

Most tuition requirements get their first matched tutor within 48 hours, so students don't lose momentum.

Our Story

Built by people who've been on both sides.

Free Tuition Media was founded in 2023 by a small team of former tutors and guardians who were frustrated with how fragmented tutor-finding was — scattered Facebook groups, unreliable agents, and no way to verify who you were actually hiring. Today the platform covers all 64 districts and connects thousands of students with tutors every month.
